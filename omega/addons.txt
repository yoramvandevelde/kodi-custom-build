# Addons bundled into the APK, on top of the 54 that Kodi ships itself.
#
# Why bundle rather than install on the device: an addon dropped into a
# profile is registered but left disabled (enabled=0 in Addons33.db), and Kodi
# does not resolve its dependencies for it either. Selecting the skin then
# loads a broken interface. Addons that ship in the source tree's addons/
# become system addons instead, which Kodi enables itself.
#
# Why the whole list rather than just the skin: skin.arctic.zephyr.mod requires
# six addons directly, and plugin.video.themoviedb.helper drags in the whole
# script.module.* row behind it. Kodi fetches none of them on its own.
#
# Versions are what the streamer ran on 2026-09-05, not "latest". A skin and
# its dependencies that are known to work together are worth more than being
# current, and this is rebuilt a few times a year at most.
#
